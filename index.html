Made in Lombard

The fabric arrives on a bolt. It leaves as your shade.

Most blind stores send your measurements to a factory in another state and wait. We cut ours in our own shop on Garfield Street. The fabric goes on the table, the tube is cut to your exact width, and there's no factory in another state to wait on. If something needs adjusting after installation, the person who made it is ten minutes away.

Cleaning & repair

The Sonic in our name.

We started as a blind-cleaning company, and the ultrasonic tank still runs in the back of the shop. Your blinds come down in the morning, labeled by window. Cavitation bubbles in the tank reach the slat joints, cord holes and headrail that a cloth never does, without bending a slat. We restring worn cords, replace broken tilters and brackets, then put the blinds back up that same afternoon. Bring them to us, or we'll come get them.

Questions

Before you call.

Is the estimate really free?
Yes. One of us comes out, measures, brings samples and leaves you a written quote. No charge, whatever you decide.
How long does installation take?
Usually one visit. We'll tell you at the estimate.
Do you clean Hunter Douglas blinds?
Yes, along with Silhouettes, wood, faux-wood, aluminum, vertical and honeycomb.
Are your shades child-safe?
Yes. Choose cordless lift, motorized, or a tensioned chain that can't form a loop. Ask and we'll spec it that way.
Do you do motorized shades?
Yes. Battery or hardwired, remote or wall switch. And we service what we install.
Do you do commercial work?
Yes. Offices and condo buildings, for cleaning or for new treatments.
